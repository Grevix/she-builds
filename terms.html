01

About the Program

SHE BUILDS is a project development and open innovation initiative organized by GREVIX. It aims to provide a structured environment in which participants can develop original ideas, collaborate with teammates, receive guidance from industry experts and work with the GREVIX team.

The program is organized around three stages:

  • Month 1: Explore. Develop, refine and present your idea while exploring its potential.
  • Month 2: Build Together. Form a team of up to four members and collaborate with support from GREVIX and mentorship opportunities with industry experts.
  • Month 3: Develop and Ship. Work toward a functional project, document your progress and prepare a final presentation or demonstration.

The exact schedule, milestones and deliverables may be communicated separately by GREVIX.

04

Project Ownership and Intellectual Property

This section is a fundamental condition of participation and must be understood before development begins.

4.1 Proposed ownership structure

For projects developed under SHE BUILDS, the proposed ownership arrangement is:

50%
GREVIX Ownership
50%
Original Project Creator

The intention is to recognize the original creator's contribution while allowing GREVIX to participate as a project co-owner and development partner.

However, the precise legal meaning of this split must be documented in a separate project agreement before any intellectual property is transferred or development begins. That agreement must identify the assets covered, the rights each party receives and the treatment of pre-existing materials.

4.2 What the ownership arrangement covers

The project agreement must expressly identify which rights are included in the proposed split, such as:

  • Project source code and software developed during the program.
  • Project-specific designs, documentation and technical materials.
  • Product concepts and other protectable intellectual property created for the project.
  • Project branding, where applicable and legally transferable.
  • Rights to maintain, modify, distribute, license or commercialize the project.

Pre-existing intellectual property, third-party materials and assets that cannot legally be transferred must be addressed separately. The 50:50 arrangement should not automatically be interpreted as transferring every asset a participant owned before joining the program.

4.3 Projects involving multiple team members

Participants may form teams of up to four members during Month 2.

Where multiple people contribute to a project, the original creator and all relevant contributors must have a written agreement clarifying their respective rights and responsibilities before substantial collaborative development begins.

The agreement must explain how the creator-side ownership allocation is handled, how contributions are recognized and whether any additional ownership interests are granted to teammates.

No participant should be assumed to have surrendered their rights merely because they contributed code, design, research or other work. The relevant rights must be clearly documented and agreed upon.

4.4 Pre-existing projects

If a participant brings an existing project into SHE BUILDS, they must identify which parts were created before the program.

The ownership agreement must distinguish between pre-existing assets and new work developed during the program. Any transfer or licensing of pre-existing intellectual property must be expressly agreed upon rather than assumed.

4.5 Copyright and legal documentation

Copyright ownership, joint ownership, assignment and licensing can have different legal consequences.

Before a project is published or commercialized under the proposed ownership arrangement, GREVIX and the relevant creators must execute an appropriate written agreement that establishes the intended rights and obligations under applicable law.
